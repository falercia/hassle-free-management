**In this post:** how to make the operation run when you are not there, using the absence rehearsal.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Nobody finds out whether the operation runs without the leader on the day the plane takes off.

Absence gets rehearsed, like anything that cannot go wrong. It starts small, half a day with no replies, and grows to two weeks. Each stage breaks something different, and that is exactly what you want, breaking it while it can still be fixed with you nearby.

What holds the rehearsal up is simple to describe and laborious to build, every area needs an owner who can answer three questions without hesitating, what the goal is, which metrics track it, and what the plan is if it goes wrong. Whoever cannot answer is not an owner, is a doer with a title.
