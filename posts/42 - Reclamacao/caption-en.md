**In this post:** how to turn a complaining team into one that proposes solutions, using the exchange counter.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

A complaining team is almost always a team that learned complaining works.

Someone brings the problem, you solve it, and the person leaves the room lighter while your list gets longer. Do that twenty times and you have become the company's complaints desk, with a queue at the door and no solutions coming out of the team. It is not laziness on their part, it is learning, you taught them that bringing the problem is enough.

The turn is not banning complaints, that only pushes dissatisfaction into the hallway. The turn is changing the currency. Here a complaint is not accepted loose, it is traded for a proposal, and whoever has no proposal takes it back with a date to return with one. The discomfort stays welcome, what changes is the format at the door.
