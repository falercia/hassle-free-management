**In this post:** individual goals or team goals, what works better, and how to find the right dose.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Individual only goals create solitary deliverers. Team only goals create free riders and heroes. The right question is not which one to pick, it is in what dose.

An individual goal gives clarity and direct ownership, which is why it works where the delivery is isolated and measurable. The price is fragmentation, everyone tends their own slice and the other person's problem stops being a problem. A team goal creates common purpose and forces people to row together, but with no clarity about each one's part it becomes dilution, and whoever carries it notices fast that they carry it alone.

The right dose is usually individual for clarity and collective for direction, tied together by a single tracking mechanism where you can see each contribution and the dependencies between them. What decides the proportion is the type of work, the more interdependent it is, the more weight the collective carries.
