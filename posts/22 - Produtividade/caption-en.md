**In this post:** how to tell whether the team is productive or just busy, using the four vital signs.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

A busy team and a productive team look very similar from the outside, and that is exactly why so many people get fooled.

Both have full calendars, both deliver tasks, both end the week tired. The difference sits in four signs almost nobody tracks, the time it takes to solve the same kind of problem, the size of the stalled queue, what the client actually notices, and how many improvements came out of the team itself.

When those four stay flat for months, you do not have a productive team, you have a team in maintenance. It works hard, solves the same things again, and the result does not move. And here comes the uncomfortable part, that is rarely laziness, it is almost always a missing yardstick, because with no clarity about what matters, effort becomes the only available metric.
