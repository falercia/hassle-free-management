**The series:** It Has a Name · Management Made Simple turns management, leadership and team topics into something anyone can understand and apply, no jargon, no loose theory, no complication. One topic per post, always with everyday analogies.

**Pillar:** 05 · Communication and Influence
**In this post:** how to say no to your boss or your client without burning the relationship, using the four nos.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Whoever says yes to everything is not collaborative, they are unreliable. Because at some point the bill arrives, and it arrives as a bad delivery.

The problem is that most people treat no as one single thing, a closed block that either comes out or does not. And there are four completely different nos, with different effects on the relationship. Not now, which is a queue question and comes with a date. Not like this, which is a scope question and comes with an alternative. Not me, which is an ownership question and comes with the right name. And not at all, the only final one, which comes with the reason.

Once you separate the four, you find that almost nothing needs the fourth. Most of what we swallow out of fear of burning a relationship was a not now with a date, or a not like this with an alternative, and nobody would have fought over it.
