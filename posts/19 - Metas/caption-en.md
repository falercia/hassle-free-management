## Suggested LinkedIn caption

An imposed goal works like a whip, it moves things for a while and then it breaks the team.

There is a huge difference between three situations that usually get treated as the same thing. The imposed goal, which the team hits as far as the pressure reaches. The announced goal, which the team understands, repeats in meetings, but does not own. And the built goal, the one the team defends when an outsider questions it.

What separates them is not the number, it is who took part in reaching it. Commitment is not imposed, it is built, and that costs one ninety minute meeting almost nobody schedules. Add a short horizon, six months works well, and an owner with a name, and the goal stops being a wish list from the board.
