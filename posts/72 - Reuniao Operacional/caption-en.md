## Suggested LinkedIn caption

An operational meeting is not a report. It is an unblocking.

It exists to keep today's work moving. Process status, what got stuck since yesterday, quick decisions so the flow does not stop. It is frequent, short, with the people who do and run the operation. The classic example is a sales team's daily, the day's target, the pipeline and the problems that need to get out of the way before lunch.

The most common mistake is turning the daily into a report. Everyone recounts what they did yesterday, one problem becomes a debate, two people argue while eight watch, and fifteen minutes become an hour. Then people start avoiding the meeting, and the blocker it was supposed to clear is still there.
