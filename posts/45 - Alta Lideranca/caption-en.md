## Suggested LinkedIn caption

There is a huge difference between taking a problem up and dumping a problem up. The first strengthens you, the second turns you into the bearer of bad news.

What separates them is the packaging. A dumped problem arrives loose, with half the context and an explanation of why it was not possible. A packaged problem arrives with four parts, the fact with no adjectives, the cost in a number, two or three options with what is gained and lost in each, and your recommendation. The board does not need you to have solved it, it needs you to have thought about it.

There is one detail that gives anyone away before the content does, the verb. Awaiting approval, depending on that team, having received no answer. Every passive verb is a responsibility you placed outside yourself. Switching to getting approval, chasing that team, pushing for the answer changes the whole reading of the presentation, because it changes who is in charge.
