**In this post:** how much a leader's behavior defines the team's, and the amplifier effect.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Everything a manager does comes out amplified by the size of the team.

You send a message at eleven at night thinking it is just a message. For the eight people who receive it, it became information about the acceptable working hours around here. You make a joke about another team in the hallway, and you just authorized eight people to do the same in meetings you will never attend. The gesture is small, the reach is not.

The problem is that the team does not copy what you declare in the values meeting, it copies what you do under pressure, when nobody is paying attention to the speech. And there are four behaviors that replicate faster than any other, how you react to a mistake, how you treat a deadline, how you talk about other teams and how you respect your own limits.
