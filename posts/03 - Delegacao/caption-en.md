## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Delegating does not take the result off your calendar. It takes the execution out of your hands.

Whoever tells the person to jump, stays in the plane watching from a distance and then demands a perfect landing, did not delegate, he dumped. Real delegation is jumping together, with the risk split.

And there is a simple yardstick, if the result vanished from your calendar the day you delegated, you outsourced accountability, and accountability does not transfer, it widens.
