## Suggested LinkedIn caption

A strategic meeting is not a long meeting. It is a meeting that chooses.

It is reserved for the big decisions, a new market, the business model, heavy investment, a risk that changes the course. Top leadership and the people with a strategic view of the company take part, less often, once a month or once a quarter, but with more depth and more impact. The agenda revolves around scenarios, trends and choices that do not get undone the following week.

The most common mistake is spending the strategic meeting on status. The leadership team opens with each area's results, the afternoon goes into numbers that fit on a dashboard, and the big topic becomes one more round of let us dig deeper. A good strategic meeting needs a briefing with scenario and data sent ahead, room for real disagreement and a firm facilitator who does not let the room leave without converging.
