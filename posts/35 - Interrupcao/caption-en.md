**Pillar:** 06 · The Leader's Time and Focus
**In this post:** how to protect the team's focus without isolating yourself, using the three doors.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

An always open door looks like generosity and is usually disorganization.

When anyone can interrupt at any hour, nobody works with depth, and worse, nobody learns to decide alone, because asking is always faster. On the other side, the manager who locks the door becomes a bunker, loses touch with reality and finds out about problems far too late.

The answer is not picking one of the extremes, it is having three doors. The open one, for what cannot wait. The one left ajar, agreed windows when you are available for questions. And the closed one, focus blocks with a clear exception rule. What makes all three work is the agreed response time, because once the team knows how long you take to answer, it stops interrupting out of insecurity.
