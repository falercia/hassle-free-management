**In this post:** how to track what was agreed without chasing anyone, using the three knots of follow up.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

If you have to send a message on Sunday to find out whether something moved, the problem is not the person, it is the missing ritual.

Follow up turns into chasing when it depends on your memory and your mood. You remember one topic, you push on that one, you forget another, you push two days later, and the team starts feeling watched at random. Random pressure creates anxiety without creating predictability, which is the worst of both worlds.

Healthy tracking rests on three knots, the agreement written and visible, a fixed review rhythm, and the signal that forces someone to warn you before it blows up. With all three, the system does the chasing, and you are free for the role only you can play, removing blockers.
