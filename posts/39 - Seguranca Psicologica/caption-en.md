**In this post:** how to get people to say what they actually think, using the scoreboard of the last person who spoke.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Nobody decides to tell the truth because of your invitation. They decide by watching what happened to the last person who spoke.

There is an invisible scoreboard running in your area. Someone brought bad news, disagreed in a meeting or flagged a risk, and the whole team watched four things, your first reaction, what happened to the idea afterward, who took the credit, and whether it cost the speaker anything. That scoreboard is updated with every episode and it decides who opens their mouth next time.

That is why psychological safety is not comfort, it is the opposite. It is creating a demanding space where disagreeing has value and no price. A comfortable team agrees with everything. A safe team argues with data and leaves with a decision.
