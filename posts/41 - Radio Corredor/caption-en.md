## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Gossip is not a character flaw in your team, it is a symptom of an information vacuum.

Where the official version is missing, an invented one moves in, and it is always worse than reality, because fear fills a gap better than hope does. There are four classic vacuums, a decision with no reason, a change with no date, a review with no criteria, and an exit with no context. Notice that all four are leadership omissions, not people's defects.

That is why turning into the police does not work. Chasing whoever talked creates a climate of surveillance, pushes the conversation into smaller groups, and you lose even the little information that still reached you. The way through is the opposite, filling the vacuum faster than the rumor can travel.
