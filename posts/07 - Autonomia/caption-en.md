## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Chaos is not born of too much freedom, it is born of people not knowing how far they can go.

A city with no traffic lights is not a free city, it is a stalled one. What gives traffic speed is precisely the clear rule, few of them, visible, the same for everyone. With a team it works the same way, autonomy without an agreed limit is not trust, it is a lottery.

The classic mistake is announcing autonomy in a meeting and changing nothing else, not the decision ceiling, not the process, not your own habit of approving. Then the first wrong call arrives, you pull everything back, and the team learns the lesson you never meant to teach, that autonomy around here lasts two weeks.
