# It Has a Name · Management Made Simple · #58 · Crisis

**Pillar:** 06 · The Leader's Time and Focus
**In this post:** the Four Orders, what to do while it burns and what can only be settled after the fire is out.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

A crisis is not managed with a plan. It is managed with orders.

In the middle of the fire, debating the best way to put it out is the fire. What works there is a short sequence: stop the damage, name an owner per front, log it while it burns, and set the date when it becomes a design topic.

And here is the part almost nobody does: the last one.

With no return on the calendar, the same emergency shows up next month under another name, and the leader who puts it out becomes the reason the fire exists.
