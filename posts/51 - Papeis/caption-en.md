# It Has a Name · Management Made Simple · #51 · Roles

**Pillar:** 03 · Execution and Process
**In this post:** the Four Line Card, how to make it clear who does what without producing yet another document nobody reads.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

A role is not defined by activity. It is defined by delivery.

Every time the boundary gets blurry, someone proposes a roles and responsibilities document. It is born complete, nobody reads it, and the real yardstick is still whoever shouts loudest. Six months later, the answer to who does what is still it depends.

The problem is not a missing document. It is that a role written by activity turns every new thing into a border dispute.
