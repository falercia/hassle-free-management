## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Authority does not come from always being right. It comes from exposing the criterion when you change.

Going back on a decision is more common than any leader admits. What separates a mature reversal from flip flopping is not the change itself, it is the reasoning being visible.

Whoever changes without showing the criterion teaches the team to doubt every decision that follows.
