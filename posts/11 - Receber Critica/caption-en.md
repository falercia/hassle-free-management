## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Criticism does not die in the content, it dies in the first ninety seconds.

Someone points out a flaw and your body reacts before your head does, the face gets warm, the explanation is ready, the context the other person did not have. You think you are clarifying, but the person across from you registers one thing only, talking to him about this is hard work.

The price comes later, and it is silent. It is not that people stop seeing your mistakes, they stop telling you. And a leader who no longer receives criticism did not get better, he went blind.
