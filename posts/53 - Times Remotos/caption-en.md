## Suggested LinkedIn caption

A remote team does not lose its pace to distance. It loses it for lack of a score.

The default reaction is raising the frequency of calls to compensate for the distance. Every call becomes a status report, because nobody prepared an agenda. The team spends the day on video and still nobody knows where anything stands.

Presence is not connection. Connection is deciding together about something real.
