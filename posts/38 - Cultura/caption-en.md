## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Culture is not what is written on the wall, it is what happens when nobody is watching.

And it does not show up all the time, it shows up concentrated in five moments. When someone fails. When someone disagrees with you. When someone asks for help. When someone is promoted. When someone leaves. What happens in those five moments teaches more about a company than any values training, because the team watches, files it away and adjusts its own behavior from there.

The cruel detail is that this learning is fast and silent. Nobody has to be told that around here you do not fail in public, they just watch what happened to the first person who did. Nobody has to read the frame to know that promotion here goes by proximity, they just watch who moved up.
