## Suggested LinkedIn caption

If you are indispensable in the detail, nobody is leading.

Micromanaging is believing only you can solve it, checking every step, approving every comma, until everything waits its turn. That is when you stop being a leader and become a walking bottleneck.

Except that stopping is not leaving the game, it is changing games. You step off the field and start deciding from the bench. A coach never steps onto the field, and still decides the match.

There is one piece of math that gives it all away, if you made the same call a hundred times last month, that is not dedication, that is a missing written rule.
