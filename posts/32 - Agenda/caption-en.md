**In this post:** how a leader should distribute their own week, using the four blocks.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Show me your last week and I will tell you whether you lead or whether you are being served.

A manager's calendar needs four blocks, and each one holds up a part of the job. Direction, which is thinking about where we are going and what the priority is. Development, which is time with the people who build the result. Decision, which is resolving what only you can resolve. And empty, the block almost nobody puts in, and which exists to absorb the unexpected without destroying the other three.

Without the fourth block, any surprise takes down the whole week, because there is no slack in the system. It is like a schedule with no margin, technically perfect and practically impossible. The difference between a leader who steers and one who runs behind is almost entirely made of margin.
