**In this post:** the Ramp, the four stretches that take a new person from arrival to their first decision alone.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Onboarding is not handing over access. It is handing over context.

We hire, unlock the tools on day one, throw the backlog over and call it integration. The person starts producing before knowing who they produce for. Thirty days later the work comes back to be redone, and the conversation turns into a lack of seniority.

It is not. It is a badly designed ramp.

There is an order, and it has four stretches: Context, Shadow, Guided and Solo. Nobody skips a stretch without paying for it later. In this carousel I show all four, and the test that tells you which stretch the person is really in, whatever the calendar says. Swipe. →
