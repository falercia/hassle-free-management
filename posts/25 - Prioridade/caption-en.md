## Suggested LinkedIn caption

If everything is urgent, nothing is urgent.

The uncomfortable part is another one, a good share of the fire you are putting out today was lit by a decision nobody made three weeks ago. That is not urgency, that is a neglect fire, and it comes back every week wearing the same face.

Prioritizing is not building a prettier list, it is having the nerve to say out loud what will not get done today. And to do that you have to separate the fire that threatens the house from the fire that only makes noise in the living room.
