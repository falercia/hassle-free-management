## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Performance reviewed without data is guesswork with a diploma.

The line is harsh, but the test is simple. Try describing someone on your team without using a single adjective. No proactive, no committed, no difficult. If nothing is left to say, you did not review that person, you formed an impression about her and then collected the examples that confirmed it.

The way out is not a longer form, it is a translation in three columns. In the first, what you think. In the second, the evidence with a date. In the third, what changes starting tomorrow. Whatever does not survive the second column should never have reached the conversation.
