# It Has a Name · Management Made Simple · #70 · Project Approval

**Pillar:** 03 · Execution and Process
**In this post:** the Grilling, four gates and eight questions a proposal has to survive before it becomes an approved project.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Most projects that go wrong were approved unanimously.

It is not a lack of intelligence in the room. It is that the whole room debates the solution and nobody interrogates the problem. Someone presents something well designed, with a timeline and a team, the conversation goes straight to the how, and whoever asks why ends up looking like the contrarian. The project enters the roadmap with the first question still unanswered, and the bill arrives six months later, when turning back is already expensive.

Approving is not agreeing. It is interrogating.

There are eight questions that separate the proposal that deserves to exist from the proposal that is merely well packaged, and they fit into four gates, the problem, the path, the price and the proof.
