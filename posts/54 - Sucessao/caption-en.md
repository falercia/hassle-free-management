**In this post:** the Four Handovers, what has to change owner for someone to genuinely take your place.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Succession does not start with choosing the person. It starts when the criterion changes owner.

The familiar script is picking someone promising, calling them a successor and carrying on deciding everything, because it is faster and safer. Two years later you have an excellent assistant and no successor.

Handing over tasks gives you time back. It builds nobody.
