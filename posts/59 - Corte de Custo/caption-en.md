## Suggested LinkedIn caption

An across the board cut is the fastest way to cut exactly what was making the difference.

When the target is the same percentage for everyone, every area hands over the piece that is easiest to remove. Nobody hands over what hurts. And what hurts is usually the very capability that holds the result up.

A cut is not a percentage. It is triage.
