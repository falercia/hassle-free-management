## Suggested LinkedIn caption

A dashboard that shows everything hides what matters.

Every team adds the number it likes, the dashboard hits a hundred and eighty metrics, and in the end nobody looks at any of them. Worse, everyone picks the number that suits the point they already wanted to make.

A good metric is not born from what already works, it is born from where it hurts. And there is a filter almost nobody applies, if the number drops and nobody knows what to do tomorrow, that is not a metric, that is decoration.
