**In this post:** how to give hard feedback without demotivating anyone, using the 4P method, Prep, Proof, Plan and Presence.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Hard feedback does not demotivate because of the content, it demotivates because of the context.

You come in throwing stones, the person closes up to defend herself, and the learning dies right there. If she walked out defensive, the one who failed was you, not her.

Before you open your mouth, answer one question, do you want her to change, or do you want to vent your frustration?
