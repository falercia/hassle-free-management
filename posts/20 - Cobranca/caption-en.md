## Suggested LinkedIn caption

There is a licence to hold people to account, and most managers push without having earned it.

The licence has three conditions, and none of them is optional. You can only demand what was agreed clearly, what the person actually controls, and what you helped unblock when she asked. Pressure that fails any of the three is not a standard, it is weight on someone who has no way to answer for it.

The difference between the manager who pulls the team up and the toxic boss is not the tone of voice, it is the preparation. One arrives with a fact, a metric and a trend, the other arrives with a feeling. One asks what is blocking, the other asks why it is not done yet. The first creates commitment, the second creates defense.
