## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Promoting the best doer is the most common way to lose a good doer and gain an unhappy manager.

The mistake is the axis you picked. Almost every promotion looks only at how much the person delivers today, when the question that matters is another one, how much she makes others deliver. Those are two different capabilities, and one does not become the other by decree or by tenure.

Cross the two axes and four profiles appear, and only one of them is a leadership promotion. The other three call for different things, a technical track, development before the leap, or an honest conversation nobody wants to have. A fair company is not one that never gets it wrong, it is one that keeps the criteria visible and stands behind the call.
