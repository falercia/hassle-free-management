**In this post:** how to keep a good professional without relying on salary, using the four anchors and the stay conversation.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Whoever stays only for the salary leaves at the first better offer, and you just created a mercenary, not a team.

Salary is the floor, not the anchor. What holds good people is something else, and there are four of them, visible growth, impact they can actually see, real autonomy, and the relationship with whoever leads them. When all four are weak, no raise fixes it, it only postpones the conversation by a few months.

There is one simple practice almost nobody runs, talking about staying before the resignation letter. It means asking, in plain words, what would make you accept an offer, and what makes you stay. Whoever asks that once every six months finds the problem while it can still be fixed.
