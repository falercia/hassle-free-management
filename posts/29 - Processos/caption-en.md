**In this post:** when to build a process and when it only gets in the way, using the process traffic light.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Making the same decision thirty times a month is not a work rhythm, it is a missing process.

There is one simple sign that the moment has come, repetition. The same decision, the same patch, the same fix, over and over. Until that happens, building a process is unnecessary anticipation, and unnecessary anticipation is bureaucracy born before the problem.

On the other side sits the process that only gets in the way, the one nobody uses, nobody understands, or that exists purely so someone can say it exists. It organizes nothing, it transfers accountability, because whoever follows the steps feels released from the result. That kind deserves to die, and killing a process is management work too.
