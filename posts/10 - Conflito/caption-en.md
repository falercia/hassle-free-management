## Suggested LinkedIn caption

An ignored conflict does not evaporate, it becomes culture.

Two people stop talking properly, each builds an island, the work starts travelling longer routes, and in three months the whole area has learned that around here we do not resolve, we detour. The cost never shows up in the spreadsheet, it shows up in the deadline.

The mediation conversation fails when the leader walks in as a judge or as a therapist. It is not about who is right, and it is not about how each one feels, it is about what happened, what it cost the result, and what has to change starting tomorrow.
