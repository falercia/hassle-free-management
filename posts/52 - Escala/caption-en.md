# It Has a Name · Management Made Simple · #52 · Scale

**Pillar:** 07 · Teams, Structure and Growth
**In this post:** the Order of the Break, the four seams that snap when a team doubles, and in what sequence.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

A team that doubles does not break on process. It breaks on an expired contract.

When the team doubles, the first thing to disappear is not the process, not the tooling and not the technology. It is shared context. Nobody knows what anyone else is working on anymore.

Next goes ownership, which turns into a fiefdom. Then the flow, and the decision that used to happen in the hallway now demands a meeting with an invite and minutes. Trust is the last one to go, and the most expensive to recover.
