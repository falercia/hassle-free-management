**In this post:** how to influence people who do not report to you, using the four currencies of influence.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Without a title you do not lose the power to influence, you only lose the easiest currency. Four remain, and all of them work better than hierarchy.

The first is delivery, the track record of doing what you promised, which is what makes someone listen before they know your argument. The second is information, when you see something another team does not, and you share it before you need anything. The third is access, you know who unblocks things, and connecting the right people creates a positive debt. The fourth is relief, you take a concrete pain out of someone's day.

And here is the part almost nobody understands, influence is a balance, not a speech. Whoever only withdraws, asking for priority, asking for urgency, asking for exceptions, goes broke in three months. Whoever deposits before needing anything has credit when the request arrives.
