## Suggested LinkedIn caption

If your criterion for leading is being the best technician in the room, you started out losing.

The expert does not want a boss who pretends to know, and spots the act in the first meeting. What the expert wants is respect for the craft, room to exercise it, and someone who owns the decision that is not technical, the one about time, cost, client and risk.

Deciding is not knowing more. Deciding is carrying the consequence. When the leader pushes that part onto the expert, that is not humility, that is becoming a hostage, and the whole team drifts because nobody wants to carry what is not theirs.
