## Suggested LinkedIn caption

The bottleneck is almost never where the water stops. It is where the water is being held back earlier.

Everyone points at the most visible stage, the one piling up work, and attacks it with more people or more overtime. Weeks later the pile reappears a bit further down, because the choke point was somewhere else. There are four places where water tends to stop in an operation, and each one calls for a completely different action.

The most uncomfortable of the four is the valve, that approval running through one person only. In most areas that person is the manager, who became the bottleneck precisely by trying to guarantee quality. In those cases the answer is not working faster, it is transferring judgment and decision rights.
