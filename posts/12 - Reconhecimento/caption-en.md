**In this post:** how to praise in a way that actually lands, using the three part praise, fact, impact and stage.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Good job is the politest way of saying you did not notice anything.

The person worked through the night, negotiated with three teams, found the way out nobody could see, and got a generic congratulations she would have heard word for word if she had done the bare minimum. It is not ingratitude, it is inattention, and the effect is the same, she concludes it makes no difference.

Praise that lands has three parts, the specific fact you saw, the impact it had on the result, and the stage, someone beyond the two of you has to know. Recognition with no audience is pleasant, but it does not become standing, and standing is the currency that makes a person want to do it again.
