## Suggested LinkedIn caption

There is a silent agreement almost every team makes and nobody owns, adapted performance.

It works like this, the person delivers half of it, the team reorganizes around that, someone covers the difference, and everyone carries on pretending things are fine. Nobody agreed to it, nobody said it, but everyone accepted it. The cost shows up in the morale of whoever carries the leftover.

Before deciding anything, an honest diagnosis is worth it, because low performance has three different causes and each one calls for a different remedy. Does not know is preparation. Cannot is condition. Will not is choice. Applying training to someone who will not, or pressure to someone who cannot, only burns your energy and the team's patience.
