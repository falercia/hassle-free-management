**In this post:** how to align expectations before they turn into frustration, using alignment in three beats.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Frustration is the gap between the film you projected and the one that played on screen.

Nobody aligns expectations after the damage, and yet that is exactly what most people do. The conversation happens once the deadline has blown, once the delivery came back different, once the client has complained. At that point there is no alignment left, only explanation.

Aligning up front costs one uncomfortable twenty minute conversation, where someone has to say out loud what counts as success, what is not included, and who they depend on to deliver. The part almost everyone skips is the risk, and if you do not share the risk, the surprise is already booked.
