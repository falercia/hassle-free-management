## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Every dismissal has three audiences, and most managers prepare the conversation for only one of them.

The first is the person leaving, who needs clarity and dignity. The second is the team that stays, which needs context and safety, because the next day everyone silently asks the same question, am I next. The third is the other teams, who will read the decision as a signal about the kind of house this company is.

When the manager takes care of the first only, productivity drops for weeks, gossip fills the space the explanation left empty, and the good people start refreshing their resumes as a precaution. A well handled dismissal is not the one nobody feels, it is the one everybody understands.
