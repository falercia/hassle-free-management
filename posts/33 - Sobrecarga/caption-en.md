**In this post:** how to get out from under when everything depends on you, using the four destinations for your list.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

When everything depends on you, the problem is no longer the volume of work, it is the design of the work.

The way out is not waking up earlier or staying later, because that math never closes. The way out is running your list through an honest triage, item by item, with four possible destinations. Delegate now, to whoever can already do it. Teach and then delegate, to whoever cannot yet. Send it back to its owner, because plenty on your list was never yours. And kill it, because part of what you do does not need to exist.

The item that hurts most is the second. Teaching costs three times the time of doing, and that is exactly why nobody teaches, and why the squeeze never ends. Whoever invests those teaching hours once buys weeks back for the rest of the year.
