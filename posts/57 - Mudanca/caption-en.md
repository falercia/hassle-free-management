**Pillar:** 04 · Culture and Behavior
**In this post:** the Beachhead, how to run a change the team did not ask for without waiting for a consensus that will never arrive.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Consensus is not the beginning of change. It is the result of it.

The trap is expecting the team to applaud a change they did not ask for. They will not. There will be resistance, there will be foot dragging, there will be people counting the days until the hurricane passes. Pretending that does not exist is the mistake that stalls everything.

What works is not convincing everyone up front. It is proving it on a small piece, with chosen people, and letting the result do the convincing.
