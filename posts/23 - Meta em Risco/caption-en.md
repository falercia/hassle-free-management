## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Halfway through the quarter there is a moment when everyone already knows the goal will not land, and nobody says a word.

The team sees it first, the manager sees it later, and the board finds out in the final week. The conversation that had to happen in May happens in June, when there is no time left to change anything. What is left is explanation, which is the expensive version of a cheap replan.

The way out is not squeezing the team harder, it is putting the math on the table. How much is missing, how much time is left, what weekly pace that demands, and what comes off the list so that pace fits. When the math does not work even in the best case, you are not managing, you are hoping, and hope is not a method.
