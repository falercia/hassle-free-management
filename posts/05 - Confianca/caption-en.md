## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Trust is not asked for, it is given.

If you are waiting for the team to prove it deserves your trust before you hand any over, you already started wrong, because distrust is a mirror, and the mirror always starts with the leader. You hold back decisions, information and risk, and the team does exactly the same to you.

Trust is not a speech either, it is a gesture. The team does not believe what you say, it believes what you release from your hands.
