**In this post:** how to align teams that keep tripping over each other, using the baton pass.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

In a relay, the baton almost never drops in the middle of the track. It drops in the exchange zone.

With teams it is the same. Each one runs well inside its own leg, everyone delivers what they consider their part, and the delay shows up exactly at the handoff, in that interval where the work has already left one hand and has not yet entered the other. Except nobody is measured on the exchange zone, each team is measured on its own leg, so the hole stays there, invisible in everyone's metrics.

Aligning is not holding more joint meetings, it is agreeing four things at every handoff, what gets passed, in what format, with how much notice and who confirms it arrived. It sounds small, but that is where most of the rework between teams lives.
