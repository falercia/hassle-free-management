## Suggested LinkedIn caption

The cost of postponing is almost always higher than the cost of getting it wrong.

Waiting for the complete report looks like prudence. Most of the time it is fear, well dressed. While the data does not arrive, the window closes, the competitor decides and your option disappears.

Deciding without everything is not guessing. It is designing the decision so the error is cheap and visible early.
