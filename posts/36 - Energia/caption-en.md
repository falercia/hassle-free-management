**Pillar:** 06 · The Leader's Time and Focus
**In this post:** how to hold a steady pace without burning out mid year, using the four beats of pace.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Whoever starts the year running a marathon at sprint pace usually stops in July.

Energy is a resource, and a resource gets managed. The classic mistake is not working too much, it is always working at the same beat, with no alternation. There is a time to push, when concentrated effort makes a difference. A time to sustain, when the work is steady and predictable. A time to recover, which almost nobody schedules. And a time to review, which is where you decide what comes off the list so the pace can fit.

The sign that you are on the wrong beat is easy to recognize. When every day feels like a push day, you do not have intensity, you have an absence of plan. And when Friday arrives and you cannot decide anything else, the problem was not the week, it was the pace chosen much earlier.
