## Suggested LinkedIn caption

Bad data is not one problem. It is four, and each one calls for something different.

Wrong, incomplete, late and disconnected. Whoever treats all four the same way always ends up in the same place: waiting for the data to improve, which is the politest way of not deciding.

The most dangerous one is not the wrong number. It is the disconnected one, because it looks right and takes the whole team in the opposite direction.
