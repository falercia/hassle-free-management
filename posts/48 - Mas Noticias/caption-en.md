**In this post:** how to communicate an unpopular decision, using the no pretending rule.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

The worst way to communicate an unpopular decision is trying to make it look good.

The team spots it in the first sentence, and from there stops listening to the content and starts assessing your sincerity. You lose two things at once, the message and the credibility, and you gain a third you did not want, the rumor mill translating the decision its own way.

An unpopular decision calls for the opposite of a sales pitch. It calls for you to name the loss before anyone asks, explain the math that led there, and hold the reaction without defending yourself. It is uncomfortable, it lasts a few minutes, and it is what separates the manager people believe from the manager everyone listens to through a filter.
