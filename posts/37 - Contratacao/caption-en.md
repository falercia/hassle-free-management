## Suggested LinkedIn caption

If you leave an interview feeling too comfortable, be suspicious. Comfort usually means you asked nothing hard.

An interview is not a talent showcase, it is an X ray of a routine. A well prepared candidate has ready made stories, and ready made stories tell you what worked, never how it was done. What reveals someone is the second layer, what exactly they did with their own hands, what went wrong, what an ordinary day really looked like, and what support they had.

Before any of that comes the part almost nobody does, defining precisely what this person has to solve in the first six months. Without it, you are not evaluating candidates, you are comparing likability, and the decision becomes a bet with no criteria.
