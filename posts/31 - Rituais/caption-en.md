**In this post:** which rituals a team actually needs, and how to tell when one of them has died.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

A ritual is not a pretty routine on the calendar. It is what keeps the team pointing at the same place when nobody is watching.

A team needs four, and only four. One for direction, to remember where we are going. One for results, to look at the number together and adjust the route. One for the hard conversation, where what is not working gets said before it turns into a grudge. And one for recognition, which is not an event, it is the practice of telling people what someone did well.

The rest is usually inheritance. A meeting someone booked under a previous manager, that nobody cancels because nobody feels like its owner. A ritual that became a dead routine charges the same time and hands nothing back, and worse, it teaches the team that things here happen by inertia.
