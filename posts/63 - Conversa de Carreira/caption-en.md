## Suggested LinkedIn caption

A vague promise motivates nobody. It only postpones the frustration to the next cycle.

The career conversation usually slips in two directions. Either the leader promises what they do not control, and it becomes a debt. Or they dodge the subject, and it becomes an absence.

There is a third path, and it starts by separating four things that are almost always said as if they were one.
