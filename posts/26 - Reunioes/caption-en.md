**In this post:** how to cut meetings without losing alignment, using the meeting swap chart.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

A recurring meeting is usually a process nobody had time to build.

When the visible number does not exist, someone books a status meeting. When accountability is unclear, someone books an alignment meeting. When a decision has no owner, someone books a meeting to decide as a group, which is the politest way of not deciding. Every meeting is born to patch a management hole, and the hole stays exactly where it was.

The way out is not cutting meetings by decree, that only pushes the problem into the hallway. The way out is replacing each type with a cheaper mechanism, and keeping on the calendar only what genuinely needs people together, which is a hard decision, a sensitive conversation, and building something jointly.
