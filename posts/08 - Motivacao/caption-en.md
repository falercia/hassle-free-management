**In this post:** what actually motivates a team day to day, using the Motivation Ladder, carrot, climate, mastery and mission.

## Suggested LinkedIn caption

The team barbecue motivates until Monday morning.

Bonus, party, a shirt with the logo, all of it is a crutch, it works for a few days and then the team lands right back where it started. It is not that any of it is wrong, it is that nobody wakes up energized because of it.

What actually moves people sits on another floor, and it has a name, pride in competence. People want to feel they are getting better at what they do, they want the challenge in their head and not in their arms, and they want to see where their effort lands in the result. When that exists, energy shows up on its own, and it does not arrive only as productivity, it arrives as a team that solves problems together.
